Yes. There is no unit to drop the car at. I bring the water, the power and the kit to your door. Tell me where the car is, home, work, wherever it sits.

I am based in the West Midlands, and I will travel further when the car is worth it.

How long does a valet take?

Full Valet is approx 1hr 30, Premium approx 2hr 30 and Luxury 3+ hours.

Exterior is from £20, Full Valet from £45, Premium from £55 and Luxury from £80. The Machine Polish package is from £150 and the Showroom Bundle is £450. Prices are a starting point, the final number depends on the car and the state it turns up in.
